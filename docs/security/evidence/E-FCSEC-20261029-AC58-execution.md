## Corrected invariant

`SecureFileOps.CopyFile` now opens `:Zone.Identifier` relative to its held source file handle. Only NT `NameNotFound` means no mark. Sharing, access and other open errors fail the copy before a stage is made. A present mark is held without write or delete sharing through publication, read to a 64 KiB limit with an extra-byte EOF check, copied to the stage, and compared byte for byte before and after the handle-relative rename. Failure removes the staged or just-published output through its held handle and reports a failed step. For an initially absent stream, the helper checks absence again immediately before and after publication. This does not freeze an absent ADS against a writer after the final check; the relevant attacker could also remove a mark from its own source before the copy. The finite claim is that an inaccessible or changing observed mark cannot silently become a committed unmarked copy.
