Capture checks the 64-root limit before allocating a selection or starting native work. It copies bounded marked store indices, including hidden marks, or the focused fallback, without a full-list selection scan. Initial preparation belongs to the captured listing generation. A stable review detaches those subscriptions; approval remains disabled until that milestone. Later navigation cannot retarget or cancel its immutable capture. Initial invalidation closes the modal, drains the actual native delegate and retires late pins without submitting.

Submission registers the original tab/location/navigation intent/mark revision before JobAdded or scheduling. Failed admission removes that registration and retires its owner. ListingModel's monotonic mark revision advances for explicit mark edits, including reversing an edit; same-location refresh restoration leaves it unchanged. Completion uses **typed storage root completeness**, independently of transfer CompletedRootIndices. Complete roots can be unmarked only when all captured origin guards still match; incomplete roots and later selections remain marked. A real native command test caught the initial use of transfer root indices, and the focused regression now explicitly leaves that transfer list empty.

Workspace refresh includes recursive descendant tabs and result tabs. Standalone Find coalesces storage completion into an existing result-tab refresh, without changing its query, location or marks. Its cached eligibility includes session completion, final timer publication, full duplicate-group publication, listing/refresh state and pending view revision. Availability reads only cached scalars. The final-publication fixtures hold both device workers so Finished alone cannot accidentally qualify pending results. Duplicate comparison retains its busy state through publication and only its current owner can retire that state.

The native transient allowance publishes a typed owning Job until its last charged handle actually retires. A waiting review distinguishes native inspection from resource waiting. When a live typed job owns that resource, **Cancel preparation and show operation** cancels/closes/drains the preparation before selecting the actual job in its main Operations pane. A pre-job owner retains ordinary Cancel and is never represented by an invented operation. Resource polling updates only wait presentation; it does not rebuild the captured disclosure or disrupt copying.
