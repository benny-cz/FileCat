for gaps. Values, keys, principal deduplication, detail strings and a complete name-map copy are conservatively
reserved before retention. Unbounded custom payloads are not retained. The process-shared default remains 512 MiB,
including existing listing indexes; row and UTF-16 caps are test-shrinkable. Retention, copied names and key references
share the character ceiling. A detached owner's buffers stay charged until its final worker/sort lease releases.

memory under a blocked native read. It includes eligible folders and counts Current, LastKnown, ordinary Absent,
Gone, Denied, detected placeholders, other Unsupported and Failed separately. Pending/retired reads stop the pass.

counts distinct retained principals in every nonresolved category. Names alone repaint without retiring observations.

Claims track publications synchronously from the beginning, exempt the operation's own Compute origin, and scope
filesystem publications to their folder. Retained results survive ordinary cache eviction. Final attachment and
accepted-sort completion check operation/store/count under the publication and store append locks. External metadata
changes retire old retention before another sort can use it; append clears the whole-listing completion claim.
